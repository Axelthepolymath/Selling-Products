Epistemology & Metaphysics

I work exclusively in epistemology and metaphysics—the study of what can be known, how knowledge is justified, and what must be true or exist for knowledge to be possible. I take difficult philosophical problems involving justification, perception, testimony, skepticism, truth, identity, causation, mind, and reality. I strip the problem down to its essential claims, examine the assumptions behind them, and develop a position that can be defended through clear reasoning and rigorous argument.

You provide a philosophical problem, argument, paper, chapter, or unresolved contradiction. I analyze it, identify the central issue, test the reasoning, and construct a rigorous response. The result explains what follows from the argument, what does not, what assumptions are required, and what must be accepted or rejected for the position to hold. You receive a written philosophical analysis and defensible argument, with the reasoning presented clearly enough to evaluate, challenge, and defend.
