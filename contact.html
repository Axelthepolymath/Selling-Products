Mohamad Wael Elhajj

I am Mohamad Wael Elhajj. Axel is the name I work under. Not a mask. A second name I chose when the first one was no longer large enough for the work.

AXEL exists because I was a devoted reader of Aristotle. I did not come to him as a tourist. I stayed. I learned how he cut a problem until only the necessary remained. Substance, cause, form, the act of knowing — those pages did not stay on a shelf. They changed the way I sit with a question.

There were nights when a sentence of his felt more like a companion than a text. I wanted a mark that could carry that seriousness into my own life: philosophy first, then mathematics, then science, then the machines that think. AXEL is that mark. A sharp letter. A quiet vow.

If you write, write as if the problem matters. I will answer in the same spirit.

— AXEL
