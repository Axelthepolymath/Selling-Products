Problem solver

PRODUCT 01

Epistemology & Metaphysics

This is not general philosophy. I take problems only in epistemology and metaphysics: what can be known, how it is known, and what must exist for that knowledge to be possible.

Typical work includes justification, perception, testimony, scepticism, truth, identity, causation, mind and world. I isolate the claim, strip the extra language, and return a position you can defend.

You bring a paper, a thesis chapter, or a contradiction you cannot dissolve. I return an argument with its costs visible: what you keep, what you give up, and why.

PRODUCT 02

Complex Mathematics

I take mathematical problems that are already hard: a proof that will not close, a structure that will not stay consistent, a model that behaves until one assumption is moved.

The work is to find the exact point of failure, rebuild the argument, and leave a result that can be checked.

You send the problem, the attempted work, and the constraint. I return definitions cleaned, steps ordered, and the remaining risk named.

PRODUCT 03

Complex Science

I take scientific problems that have outgrown a simple experiment or a single formula: mixed data, a model that will not predict, a mechanism with too many moving parts.

The work is to name the phenomenon, cut what does not matter, and design a line of attack that can be tested.

You send the question, the evidence you already have, and the limit you are under. I return a model of the problem and the next move that would actually change it.
